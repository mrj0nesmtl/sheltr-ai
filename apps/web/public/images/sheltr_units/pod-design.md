SHELTR Technical Specifications & Materials Document

1. Overview

SHELTR mobile micro-housing units are designed to provide safe, insulated, and weather-resistant accommodations for one or two occupants. Each unit is fabricated with mobility in mind (caster wheels or optional bike hitch) and incorporates sustainable power via solar energy, a small water tank, and emergency sanitation. Units are engineered for cold-weather use and rated for Canadian and northern U.S. winters.

⸻

2. Unit Types

Model A: SHELTR One-Person Unit
	•	Exterior Dimensions: approx. 7 ft (L) × 4 ft (W) × 6.5 ft (H)
	•	Interior Space: ~28 sq. ft. usable floor area
	•	Occupancy: 1 person

Model B: SHELTR Two-Person Unit
	•	Exterior Dimensions: approx. 12 ft (L) × 6 ft (W) × 7 ft (H)
	•	Interior Space: ~72 sq. ft. usable floor area
	•	Occupancy: 2 persons

⸻

3. Structural Materials
	•	Frame: Welded lightweight aluminum square tubing (powder-coated for corrosion resistance)
	•	Panels: Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs) made from:
	•	Exterior skin: powder-coated galvanized steel or aluminum composite panels (ACP)
	•	Insulation core: rigid closed-cell polyurethane (R-value ≈ R-20 for walls, R-30 for roof)
	•	Interior lining: marine-grade plywood with water-resistant laminate finish
	•	Roofing: EPDM rubber membrane with solar panel mount and optional vent/air-conditioning unit
	•	Flooring:
	•	Steel-reinforced subfloor with vapor barrier
	•	Insulated marine-grade plywood
	•	Anti-slip vinyl composite tile finish

⸻

4. Insulation & Winter Rating
	•	Rated for -25°C / -13°F sustained temperatures
	•	Wall thickness: 2–3 inches (SIP panels with vapor barrier)
	•	Weather-sealed windows (double-pane polycarbonate, low-E coating)
	•	Gasketed insulated entry door with deadbolt

⸻

5. Power & Electrical
	•	Solar Panel: 200–300W rooftop array (expandable)
	•	Battery Storage: 12V deep-cycle AGM or LiFePO4 (100Ah minimum)
	•	Inverter/Charger: 12V to 110V inverter (600–1000W capacity)
	•	Outlets: At least one 110V AC outlet and two USB charging ports
	•	Lighting: Low-wattage LED interior light (1–2 fixtures)

⸻

6. Water & Sanitation
	•	Water Tank:
	•	One-person unit: 10L capacity
	•	Two-person unit: 20L capacity
	•	Pump: 12V low-flow pump to sink
	•	Sink: Compact stainless steel single-basin with folding faucet
	•	Porta Potty:
	•	5L chemical-based unit
	•	Concealed beneath trunk seat (Model A) or under desk (Model B)

⸻

7. Heating & Ventilation
	•	Ventilation: Roof-mounted low-energy fan + optional 12V A/C + heater combo
	•	Heating Options:
	•	Model A: 12V ceramic heater (powered by battery + solar)
	•	Model B: propane-compatible vented heater (optional upgrade)
	•	Airflow: Two operable windows for passive cross-ventilation

⸻

8. Interior Layout

Model A: One-Person
	•	Single bed with storage trunk underneath (conceals Porta Potty)
	•	Folding wall-mounted desk
	•	Compact shelving

Model B: Two-Person
	•	Two single beds or one full-size bed with under-bed storage
	•	Desk with integrated shelving
	•	Laundry washer/dryer combo (compact 24”)
	•	Sink module with water tank and drain system

⸻

9. Mobility & Anchoring
	•	Mobility: Heavy-duty caster wheels with locking mechanism
	•	Transport: Optional bicycle hitch or tow dolly adapter
	•	Anchoring: Retractable steel stabilizer legs for stationary placement

⸻

10. Branding & Technology
	•	Exterior Finish: Matte dark gray powder-coated panels
	•	Branding: SHELTR logo + QR code on entry side
	•	Smart Add-ons (optional):
	•	WiFi hotspot
	•	Bluetooth-controlled lighting

⸻

11. Safety & Compliance
	•	Fire-retardant interior panels (NFPA 701 compliant)
	•	Locking insulated door with safety release
	•	Smoke/CO2 detector
	•	GFCI-protected electrical outlet

⸻

✅ Summary:
The one-person unit prioritizes compact emergency shelter with minimal systems.
The two-person unit expands space, integrates a workstation, washer/dryer, and larger water capacity.
Both are fully insulated, solar-powered, and winter-rated, ensuring survival and dignity for their users.
