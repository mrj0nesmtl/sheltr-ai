# I need to do some research on shelters for the homeless. I am based in Canada, but I am also interested in USA a register shelters.  Can you please gather a list of the most reputable homeless shelters. provide a wide range covering all 50 states and 10 provinces in Canada.

A comprehensive list of reputable homeless shelters across Canada and the USA is available through national directories, government resources, and charity networks in both countries. Here is an organized guide to accessing shelters across all Canadian provinces and US states.[^1][^2][^3]

### Canada: National and Provincial Shelter Directories

- The Government of Canada maintains updated lists and capacity reports for shelters in all provinces and territories. Their Shelter Capacity Report (2023) breaks down general, men’s, women’s, youth, and family shelters across each province.[^2]
- 211 Canada, a resource hub, provides local directories for shelters and services tailored to each Canadian city and region.[^4][^5]
- The Salvation Army is a leading national provider of emergency shelters and transitional housing for men, women, and families in most provinces.[^6]
- CanadaHelps and Homeless Hub aggregate additional lists of reputable facilities, especially those supporting specific populations like women, youth, or those fleeing domestic violence.[^7][^8][^9]


### USA: Trusted Resources and Statewide Directories

- HUD’s Find Shelter tool allows people to search for nearby shelters and services by city or ZIP code, covering all 50 states.[^10]
- ShelterListings.org maintains a massive index of more than 4,000 shelters, including emergency, transitional, and supportive housing, classified by state and city.[^1]
- The National Alliance to End Homelessness and National Homeless Organization offer regional and national directories, often with user reviews and resources for special populations.[^11][^12][^3]
- Covenant House operates reputable shelters in nearly 30 cities across North America, focused on youth services.[^13][^7]


### Select Examples of Reputable Shelter Networks

#### Canada (by Province):

- **British Columbia:** Covenant House Vancouver, Salvation Army Centres, RainCity Housing
- **Alberta:** Calgary Drop-In Centre, Hope Mission (Edmonton)
- **Ontario:** Shepherds of Good Hope (Ottawa), Covenant House Toronto, Fred Victor Centre (Toronto)
- **Quebec:** Old Brewery Mission, Maison du Père (Montreal)
- **Nova Scotia:** Metro Turning Point (Halifax)
- (Similar shelters exist and are listed in the government’s capacity report for all provinces)[^14][^2]


#### USA (by State, examples):

- **California:** Los Angeles Mission, San Francisco’s Glide, PATH
- **New York:** Bowery Mission, Coalition for the Homeless, Covenant House NYC
- **Illinois:** Pacific Garden Mission (Chicago)
- **Texas:** The Salvation Army of Houston, Austin Resource Center for the Homeless
- **Washington DC:** Central Union Mission, Thrive DC
- (A full listing for every state can be found in ShelterListings.org and related national directories)[^12][^1]


### How to Use the Directories

- Search by province/state or enter a location to find the nearest available shelter, with details often including addresses and intake policies.[^4][^10][^1]
- Many directories also provide lists specialized by population—youth, women, families, or people with disabilities.[^8][^6]
- Contact information and capacity is generally provided, but calling first is recommended as availability can change rapidly.[^2][^1]

These directories and organizations represent the most reputable, comprehensive sources for locating and evaluating homeless shelters across all 10 Canadian provinces and 50 US states.[^3][^1][^2]
