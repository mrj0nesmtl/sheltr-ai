# MSB Registration & Incorporation Guide for SHELTR
**Regulatory Compliance for Crypto-Enabled Donation Platform**

---

## Executive Summary

SHELTR's donation platform, which invests a percentage of donations into a crypto high-yield staking institutional fund, **requires registration as a Money Service Business (MSB)** in Canada under FINTRAC regulations. This document outlines the regulatory requirements, recommended incorporation structure, and next steps.

---

## Question

**Joel's Query:**
> I'm building an Internet donation platform and our company is not yet registered. A feature of our donation platform is that it invests a percentage of the donation into a crypto high-yield staking institutional fund. Do I have to be registered as a money service business? What kind of incorporation should I register as and where?

**Source:** [Perplexity AI Conversation](https://www.perplexity.ai/search/0697927b-55ad-4f5d-bd01-e295e55f0abc)

---

## Answer Summary

**Yes**, SHELTR must register as a Money Service Business (MSB) in Canada. The platform's activities—specifically "dealing in virtual currency" and operating as a "crowdfunding platform service"—fall directly under FINTRAC's MSB regulations.

---

## MSB Registration Requirements

### 1. **Mandatory FINTRAC Registration**

- **Required Before Operations**: Registration with FINTRAC is mandatory **prior to commencing operations** if the platform:
  - Deals in cryptocurrency
  - Handles user funds for crypto investments
  - Invests on behalf of donors
  
- **Covered Activities**:
  - Dealing in virtual currency
  - Crowdfunding platform services
  - Investment or staking on behalf of users

### 2. **Compliance Program Requirements**

SHELTR must implement and maintain:

✅ **Compliance Officer**: Appoint a designated compliance officer  
✅ **AML/KYC Program**: Anti-Money Laundering and Know Your Customer procedures  
✅ **Transaction Reporting**: Robust mechanisms for transaction reporting  
✅ **Record-Keeping**: Comprehensive record-keeping systems  
✅ **Risk Assessment**: Ongoing risk assessment and mitigation

### 3. **Physical Presence Requirement**

- **Canadian MSB**: Physical presence in Canada is generally required for full MSB registration
- **Foreign MSB (FMSB)**: Alternative option for foreign entities actively serving Canadian residents
- **SHELTR Decision Point**: Determine whether to establish Canadian operations or proceed as FMSB

**Source:** [Crypto License in Canada 2025](https://legalaes.com/crypto-license-in-canada/)

## Recommended Incorporation Structure

### **Corporate Structure**

**Best Option**: **Limited Company (LTD or INC)**

- Federally incorporated OR provincially incorporated
- Most common and flexible structure for fintech/crypto platforms
- Provides liability protection and credibility
- Facilitates future fundraising and partnerships

## Next Steps for SHELTR

### **Phase 1: Pre-Registration (Immediate)**

1. **Decide Incorporation Jurisdiction**
   - [ ] Evaluate Ontario vs. Alberta based on strategic priorities
   - [ ] Consider federal vs. provincial incorporation
   
2. **Incorporate the Company**
   - [ ] File incorporation documents (LTD or INC)
   - [ ] Obtain Business Number (BN) from CRA
   - [ ] Register for GST/HST if applicable

3. **Establish Corporate Governance**
   - [ ] Appoint directors and officers
   - [ ] Create shareholder agreement
   - [ ] Set up corporate bank account

---

### **Phase 2: MSB Registration (Before Operations)**

4. **Prepare Compliance Framework**
   - [ ] Appoint Compliance Officer
   - [ ] Develop AML/KYC policies and procedures
   - [ ] Create transaction monitoring systems
   - [ ] Establish record-keeping protocols
   - [ ] Conduct initial risk assessment

5. **Submit FINTRAC Registration**
   - [ ] Complete MSB registration application
   - [ ] Submit required documentation
   - [ ] Pay registration fees
   - [ ] Obtain registration certificate

6. **Implement Compliance Systems**
   - [ ] Set up Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR) process
   - [ ] Implement Large Cash Transaction Reporting (LCTR)
   - [ ] Create Terrorist Financing (TF) reporting procedures
   - [ ] Train all staff on AML/KYC requirements

### **Phase 3: Operational Readiness**

7. **Crypto Partnership Setup**
   - [ ] Select institutional staking partner (must be compliant)
   - [ ] Establish custody arrangements
   - [ ] Set up secure wallets and multi-sig protocols
   - [ ] Implement audit trails for all crypto transactions

   - [ ] Engage legal counsel specializing in fintech/crypto
   - [ ] Review terms of service and donation agreements
   - [ ] Ensure securities law compliance (if applicable)

9. **Insurance & Risk Management**
   - [ ] Obtain cyber liability insurance

   - [ ] Implement cybersecurity protocols

### 📋 **Ongoing Obligations**

Once registered as an MSB, SHELTR must:

- **Report** suspicious transactions within prescribed timelines
- **File** annual compliance reports with FINTRAC
- **Maintain** records for minimum 5 years
- **Update** registration within 30 days of material changes
- **Train** employees regularly on AML/KYC
- **Conduct** periodic risk assessments

## Foreign MSB (FMSB) Alternative

If SHELTR incorporates outside Canada but serves Canadian residents:

### **FMSB Requirements:**
- Register as Foreign Money Services Business
- Appoint Canadian agent or representative
- Maintain Canadian compliance program
- File reports with FINTRAC
- May be subject to additional scrutiny

### **When to Consider FMSB:**
- Primary operations are outside Canada
- Serving global market with Canadian users
- Strategic reasons for foreign incorporation (e.g., Delaware, Cayman)
