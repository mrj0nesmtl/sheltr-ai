## 🏕️ Overview

Basecamp is SHELTR's innovative community support hub that transforms shipping containers into 24/7 staffed centers providing essential services, technical assistance, and social support for POD communities. When SHELTR deploys emergency housing to a city, the PODs arrive inside a Basecamp container—which then remains on-site as permanent infrastructure, creating a supported community rather than isolated housing units.

---

## 📦 The Basecamp Concept

### From Logistics to Infrastructure

**The Innovation**: Turn the shipping container into the solution, not just the delivery method.

1. **Initial Deployment**
   - PODs arrive in a 40ft high cube shipping container
   - Container from ATS Containers (Ontario partnership)
   - Specialized side-door configuration for compartmentalized access

2. **Transformation**
   - Container remains on-site with proper permissions/legal agreements
   - Transforms into permanent community support center
   - Side doors create separate service areas

3. **Community Formation**
   - PODs set up nearby (walking distance)
   - Centralized services and support
   - 24/7 staffed volunteer coordination
   - Emergency response capabilities

## 🛠️ Container Specifications

### ATS Containers - 40ft High Cube Side Door

**Supplier**: [ATS Containers](https://www.atscontainers.com/en/products/new-containers/product/40-new-high-cube-with-side-doors/)  
**Location**: Ontario, Canada  
**Model**: 40ft High Cube with Side Doors

#### Technical Specifications

**Dimensions**:
- **Exterior**: 40'L × 8'W × 9'6"H
- **Interior**: 39'5"L × 7'6"W × 8'10"H
- **Door Opening**: 7'8"W × 8'5"H
- **Side Door Openings**: 7'8"W × 8'5"H (4 OEM doors on right side)

**Construction**:
- **Material**: CORTEN anti-corrosive steel
- **Floor**: 19 plies Keruing-Apitong marine grade plywood (1.1" / 28mm)
- **Condition**: One-trip (new) container
- **Security**: Lockbox on each door set, 36" high locking bar door handles

**Capacity**:
- **Weight**: 9,390 lbs
- **Payload**: 43,520 lbs

#### Why This Container?

1. **Side Door Access**: 4 OEM doors create compartmentalized service areas
2. **High Cube**: Extra height (9'6") for better interior space
3. **Marine Grade**: Durable construction withstands frequent use
4. **Security**: Built-in lockboxes for each section
5. **Canadian Made**: Domestic supply chain, faster deployment

## 💰 Funding Model

### How Basecamp is Funded

**SmartFund™ Allocation**:
- **15% Housing Fund**: Covers POD deployment AND Basecamp operations
- **Staking Yields**: 4-6% APY on housing fund (Coinbase)
- **Returns**: Fund Basecamp staffing, supplies, maintenance
- **Efficiency**: Flat-pack design reduces shipping costs by 50%

### Cost Breakdown

**One-Time Costs**:
- Container: ~$8,000-$10,000 CAD (40ft HC side door)
- Setup: Site preparation, permits, utilities
- Equipment: Computers, appliances, furniture

**Ongoing Costs**:
- Volunteer Coordination: Stipends, training
- Utilities: Power, water, internet
- Supplies: Food, detergent, medical supplies
- Maintenance: Repairs, cleaning, security

**Funded By**:
- Housing fund allocation (15% of all donations)
- Staking yields on housing fund
- Community partnerships and grants
- In-kind donations (food, supplies)

### Daily Operations

#### Morning (7am-12pm)
- Security shift change
- Breakfast service (7am-9am)

#### Afternoon (12pm-5pm)

#### Evening (5pm-10pm)
- Dinner service (5pm-7pm)
- Evening programs
- Support groups
- Computer lab (extended hours)
- Security rounds
- Facility maintenance

#### Overnight (10pm-7am)
- Security staff on duty
- Emergency response available
- WiFi access continues
- Basic assistance as needed
- Facility monitoring

### Emergency Protocols

**Medical Emergency**:
1. Call 911 immediately
2. Notify on-site medical staff
3. Provide first aid if trained
4. Document incident
5. Follow-up with participant

**Security Incident**:
1. Ensure safety of all participants
2. Contact security staff
3. Call police if needed
4. Document incident
5. Review and adjust protocols

**POD Emergency**:
1. Assess situation (power, climate, security)
2. Dispatch technical support
3. Provide temporary accommodation if needed
4. Repair or replace systems
5. Follow-up with participant
