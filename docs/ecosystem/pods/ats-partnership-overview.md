# ATS Containers Partnership Overview

**Manufacturing Partnership for SHELTR Model A Flat-Pack**

*Version: 1.0 | Updated: December 11, 2025*

## 🎯 Partnership Overview

SHELTR has partnered with **ATS Containers** (Ontario, Canada) as the primary manufacturing partner for the Model A flat-pack emergency housing system. This strategic partnership brings world-class modular container design expertise and ISO-certified manufacturing to the SHELTR platform.

**Partnership Focus**: Flat-pack engineering, aluminum frame fabrication, SIP panel manufacturing, and quality assurance.

## 🤝 Partnership Scope

### SHELTR Model A Responsibilities

**Frame Manufacturing:**
- **Aluminum Frame Sections**: Precision-cut 6063-T5 aluminum square tubing
  - 12 frame sections per unit
  - Pre-drilled mounting holes
  - Powder-coated finish (matte dark gray)
  - Labeled for easy assembly (A-L)

- **Corner Connectors & Brackets**: Precision-machined aluminum
  - 8 corner connectors per unit
  - Load-bearing capacity: 1.5x safety factor
  - Pre-drilled and labeled
  - Corrosion-resistant finish

- **Stabilizer Legs**: Retractable steel construction
  - 4 legs per unit
  - Adjustable height (6-12 inches)
  - Locking mechanism
  - Load capacity: 200 lbs each

**Composite Wall Panel Manufacturing:**
- **Wall Panels**: 4 modular composite units per POD
  - Core: 2" EPS (Expanded Polystyrene) foam (R-20)
  - Coating: Polyurea spray-applied on both sides
  - Reinforcement: Steel plates bonded to exterior and interior faces
  - Finish: Smooth coating over steel plates
  - Pre-installed gaskets and sealing systems
  - Labeled for assembly sequence (1-4)

- **Roof Panels**: 2 modular insulated units per POD
  - Enhanced insulation (R-30, 4" polyisocyanurate foam)
  - Integrated EPDM rubber membrane
  - Solar panel mounting points
  - Pre-applied weather sealing
  - Labeled (R1-R2)

- **Floor Panel**: 1 insulated unit per POD
  - Steel-reinforced subfloor
  - Insulation (R-10, 2" XPS foam)
  - Anti-slip vinyl finish
  - Vapor barrier integrated
  - Labeled (F1)

**Door & Window Integration:**
- Pre-hung insulated door assembly
- Double-pane polycarbonate windows
- Weather-sealed gaskets
- Smart lock mounting preparation
- Hardware included

**Quality Control:**
- 100% component inspection
- Structural load testing
- Weather sealing verification
- Dimensional accuracy checks
- Pre-assembly fit testing

**Packaging & Logistics:**
- Flat-pack pallet configuration (3 pallets per unit)
- Weather-protected packaging
- Component labeling and documentation
- Shipping optimization
- Inventory management

## 🏗️ Manufacturing Process

### Design & Engineering Phase

**1. Collaborative Design:**
- SHELTR provides specifications and requirements
- ATS engineers develop manufacturing drawings
- Iterative design optimization
- Prototype development and testing
- Final design approval

**2. Tooling & Setup:**
- Custom tooling for aluminum fabrication
- SIP panel production setup
- Quality control fixtures
- Assembly jigs and templates
- Testing equipment calibration

### Production Phase

**3. Aluminum Frame Fabrication:**
- Precision cutting of aluminum tubing
- CNC drilling of mounting holes
- Deburring and finishing
- Powder coating application
- Quality inspection and labeling

**4. Composite Wall Panel Production:**
- EPS foam core cutting and preparation
- Steel plate bonding to foam core (both sides)
- Polyurea spray coating application (both sides)
- Curing and quality inspection
- Edge sealing and gasket installation
- Dimensional verification

**5. Door & Window Assembly:**
- Door pre-hanging and hardware installation
- Window integration into panels
- Weather sealing application
- Smart lock mounting preparation
- Operation testing

**6. Quality Assurance:**
- Visual inspection (100% of components)
- Dimensional accuracy verification
- Load testing (sample basis)
- Weather sealing testing
- Pre-assembly fit check

**7. Packaging & Shipping:**
- Component organization by pallet
- Protective packaging application
- Labeling and documentation
- Pallet securing and wrapping
- Shipping coordination
