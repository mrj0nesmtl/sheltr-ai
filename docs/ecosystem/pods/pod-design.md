# POD Design Specifications - Flat-Pack Model A

**Version: 3.0 | Updated: December 11, 2025**

---

## 1. Overview

The SHELTR Model A is a revolutionary flat-pack modular emergency housing unit designed for rapid deployment, dignified living, and sustainable operation. Inspired by IKEA-style flat-pack furniture but engineered for extreme weather conditions, the Model A ships disassembled and assembles in 2-4 hours with two people and basic tools.

**Design Philosophy**: One Model. Infinite Possibilities.

The flat-pack approach delivers:
- 60% reduced shipping volume
- Simplified manufacturing (single SKU)
- Faster deployment (no heavy equipment)
- Lower cost ($10,000-$12,000 CAD)
- Easier logistics (standard pallet shipping)
- Modular expansion capability

Units are engineered for Canadian and northern climates, rated for -25°C / -13°F sustained operation.

## 3. Flat-Pack Panel System

### Advanced Composite Wall Panels

The Model A uses pre-fabricated composite panels that snap together for rapid assembly while maintaining superior insulation, structural integrity, and enhanced durability.

**Panel Specifications:**

**Wall Panels (4 units)**
- **Dimensions**: 7 ft (H) × 4 ft (W) × 2 in (thickness)
- **Core Construction**: 2" EPS (Expanded Polystyrene) foam
  - R-value: R-20 thermal performance
  - Fire-retardant treatment (NFPA 701 compliant)
  - Lightweight and moisture-resistant
- **Polyurea Coating**: Spray-applied on both sides
  - Waterproof barrier
  - Impact-resistant and flexible
  - UV-resistant
  - Chemical-resistant
  - Matte dark gray finish standard
- **Steel Plate Reinforcement**: Bonded to exterior and interior faces
  - Enhanced structural strength
  - Impact resistance: 250 J/m
  - Corrosion-resistant treatment
  - Load-bearing capacity increased by 40%
- **Interior Finish**: Smooth white coating over steel plate
  - Easy to clean
  - Mold-resistant
  - Durable surface

**Roof Panels (2 units)**
- **Dimensions**: 7 ft (L) × 2 ft (W) × 4 in (thickness)
- **Insulation**: R-30 thermal value (4" polyisocyanurate foam)
- **Roofing Membrane**: EPDM rubber
  - 20-year warranty
  - Solar panel mounting integrated
  - Ventilation unit mounting points
- **Weather Sealing**: Pre-applied gaskets and sealing tape

**Floor Panel (1 unit)**
- **Dimensions**: 7 ft (L) × 4 ft (W) × 2 in (thickness)
- **Construction**: Steel-reinforced subfloor with vapor barrier
- **Insulation**: R-10 (2" XPS foam)
- **Finish**: Anti-slip vinyl composite tile (VCT)
  - Commercial-grade durability
  - Easy to clean
  - Sound-dampening underlayment

### Panel Connection System

**Snap-Fit & Bolt Assembly:**
- Interlocking edge profiles for alignment
- Weather-sealed gaskets (pre-installed)
- Tamper-evident fasteners
- Tool-free initial positioning
- Weatherproof bolt securing
- Color-coded assembly markings
