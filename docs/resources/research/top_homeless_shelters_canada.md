# Compile a list of top homeless shelters in each Canadian province

Here is a province-by-province list of some of the top and most reputable homeless shelters across Canada. This initial list focuses on British Columbia, Alberta, and Saskatchewan, with notable, well-established shelters and resources named for each province.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

### British Columbia

- **Lookout Housing and Health Society (Multi-city):** Offers a wide range of shelter beds and support services in Vancouver, Victoria, Surrey, New Westminster, and more.[^8]
- **Covenant House Vancouver:** Dedicated youth shelter and support in Vancouver.[^9]
- **Richmond House Emergency Shelter (Richmond):** Provides temporary accommodation, meals, and supports.[^10]
- **MCSS Emergency Shelter (Mission):** 57-bed shelter with extensive services and advocacy.[^1]
- **Hearthstone Place (Abbotsford):** Supportive housing program with individual units.[^6]


### Alberta

- **Calgary Drop-In \& Rehab Centre (Calgary):** One of the largest in Alberta, providing year-round emergency shelter.[^2][^11]
- **Hope Mission Herb Jamieson Centre (Edmonton):** 24/7 facility with specialized services for men and those with mobility challenges.[^4]
- **Alpha House Shelter (Calgary):** Emergency shelter focusing on addiction support.[^2]
- **Inn From the Cold (Calgary):** Family-focused shelter.[^2]
- **Mustard Seed (multiple Alberta locations):** Emergency shelters and support services in Calgary, Red Deer, Lethbridge, Medicine Hat.[^2]
- **Wapiti House (Grande Prairie):** Year-round shelter serving northern Alberta.[^2]


### Saskatchewan

- **The Mustard Seed Saskatoon:** 40-bed, 24/7 emergency shelter with wraparound supports; set to expand further.[^3][^5]
- **Salvation Army Saskatoon:** Emergency shelter and after-hours services for men.[^12]
- **YWCA Regina \& Saskatoon:** Crisis shelter and residence for women and children.[^7]
- **Qu'Appelle Haven Safe Shelter; West Central Crisis \& Family Support Centre (Various):** Noted regional shelters in Saskatchewan.[^7]

This list emphasizes high-capacity, well-known, and widely referenced shelters serving a diverse range of populations in each province. Additional directories such as ShelterSafe and 211 services can be used to find more beds and specialized accommodations in every region.[^13][^14][^15]
