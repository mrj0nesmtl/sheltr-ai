## 👼 Strategy 3: Angel Investors ($50K Target)

### **What Are Angel Investors?**

**Definition**: High-net-worth individuals who invest their own money in early-stage startups.

**Characteristics**:
- ✅ **Fast Decisions**: Can decide in days/weeks (not months)
- ✅ **Flexible Terms**: More negotiable than VCs
- ✅ **Hands-On**: Often provide mentorship, intros
- ✅ **Smaller Checks**: $5K-50K (vs. $500K+ for VCs)
- ❌ **Less Capital**: Can't write big checks
- ❌ **Variable Quality**: Some are great, some not

### **Types of Angels**

#### **1. Tech Angels**

**Characteristics**:
- Former founders, executives, engineers
- Understand tech, product, scaling
- Can provide technical advice
- Network in tech ecosystem

**Where to Find**:
- AngelList (https://angel.co/)
- LinkedIn (search "angel investor" + "tech")
- Tech conferences, meetups
- Accelerator demo days

#### **2. Impact Angels**

**Characteristics**:
- Care about social/environmental impact
- Often from non-profit, philanthropy backgrounds
- May accept lower returns for higher impact
- Network in impact ecosystem

**Where to Find**:
- Impact investor networks
- Social enterprise conferences
- Non-profit galas, events
- Through foundations, accelerators

#### **3. Crypto Angels**

**Characteristics**:
- Made money in crypto/blockchain
- Understand crypto, DeFi, Web3
- May invest in crypto (not just fiat)
- Network in crypto ecosystem

**Where to Find**:
- Crypto Twitter, Discord, Telegram
- Blockchain conferences (Consensus, ETHDenver)
- Crypto angel networks
- Through crypto VCs, accelerators

#### **4. Local Angels**

**Characteristics**:
- Based in your city/region
- Want to support local ecosystem

- Easier to meet in person

**Where to Find**:
- Local angel networks (e.g., Vancouver Angel Forum)

- Startup community events
- Through local VCs, accelerators

## 🎯 Pitch Strategy

### **Tailoring Your Pitch**

**For Grants**:
- ✅ Emphasize **impact** (# housed, lives changed)
- ✅ Highlight **innovation** (blockchain, AI, unique)

- ✅ Demonstrate **traction** (pilot projects, partnerships)

- ✅ Highlight **opportunity** (big market, unique solution)
- ✅ Demonstrate **progress** (beta platform, partnerships)
- ✅ Show **momentum** (other investors, grants, press)

## 💡 Pro Tips for Fundraising Success

### **1. Start with Warm Introductions**

**Why**:
- ✅ 10x higher response rate than cold emails
- ✅ Investor trusts introducer's judgment
- ✅ Faster to meeting

**How**:
- Ask advisors, mentors, existing investors for intros
- Use LinkedIn to find mutual connections
- Attend events where investors are present
- Join accelerators, incubators (built-in intros)

### **2. Create FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)**

**Why**:
- ✅ Investors move faster when they see momentum
- ✅ Creates competitive dynamic
- ✅ Better terms (higher valuation, less dilution)

**How**:
- Announce grant wins publicly (Twitter, LinkedIn, press)
- Share investor commitments ("We have $100K committed, raising $150K more")
- Set deadlines ("Closing round on Dec 31")
- Show traction (pilot launches, partnerships, press)

### **3. Be Transparent About Challenges**

**Why**:
- ✅ Builds trust (investors appreciate honesty)
- ✅ Avoids surprises later (due diligence will find issues anyway)
- ✅ Shows self-awareness (you know the risks)

**How**:
- Acknowledge challenges in pitch ("We're pre-revenue, but...")
- Explain how you'll overcome them ("We have 3 pilots launching next month...")
- Show you've thought through risks ("If pilot fails, we'll pivot to...")

### **4. Follow Up Consistently**

**Why**:
- ✅ Investors are busy, need reminders
- ✅ Shows persistence, commitment
- ✅ Keeps you top of mind

**How**:
- Send monthly investor updates (even before they invest)
- Share traction milestones (pilot launch, partnership, press)
- Ask for intros to other investors
- Be helpful (share relevant articles, intros)

### **5. Don't Take Rejection Personally**

**Why**:
- ✅ Fundraising is a numbers game (90% will say no)
- ✅ "No" often means "not now" (timing, thesis, portfolio)
- ✅ Keeps you motivated

**How**:
- Ask for feedback ("What would need to change for you to invest?")
- Stay in touch (send updates, may invest in next round)
- Move on quickly (don't dwell, focus on next investor)
