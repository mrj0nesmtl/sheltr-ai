## Marketing Philosophy

Christine's approach to marketing SHELTR is guided by core principles:

### 1. **Authenticity First**
Every message must be genuine and true to SHELTR's mission. No hype, no exaggeration—just honest storytelling about real impact.

### 2. **Empathy Always**
Understanding the perspectives of participants, shelters, donors, and all stakeholders. Communications must be sensitive, respectful, and human-centered.

### 3. **Clarity & Accessibility**
Complex technology and social systems must be communicated simply. If it's not clear, it's not effective.

### 4. **Multi-Medium Storytelling**
Using the right medium for the right message—visual, written, video, audio—to reach audiences where they are and how they prefer to engage.

### 5. **Data-Driven Creativity**
Balancing creative intuition with analytical rigor. Testing, measuring, and optimizing while maintaining artistic integrity.
