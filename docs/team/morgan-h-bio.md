## Design Philosophy

Mo's approach to participant support services is guided by core principles:

### 1. **Dignity First**
Every design decision must respect the dignity and autonomy of participants. Technology should empower, not stigmatize.

### 2. **Accessibility Always**
If it doesn't work for everyone, it doesn't work. Designs must be accessible to users with varying abilities, literacy levels, and technical experience.

### 3. **Empathy-Driven**
Understanding comes from listening. Mo ensures participant voices guide every design decision through research, testing, and continuous feedback.

### 4. **Simplicity & Clarity**
Complex problems require simple solutions. Interfaces should be intuitive, reducing barriers to access rather than creating them.

### 5. **Human Connection**
Technology should enhance human relationships, not replace them. SHELTR's systems support case managers, counselors, and service providers in building meaningful connections with participants.
