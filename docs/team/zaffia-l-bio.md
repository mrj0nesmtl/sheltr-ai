## About Zaffia

Zaffia Laplante is an Indigenous leader and startup founder based in Canada, recognized for her role as CEO of Hempergy, a cleantech startup based in Waterloo, Ontario. She is a member of the Métis Nation of Ontario and has been deeply involved in creating sustainable solutions for Indigenous communities, particularly through upcycled hemp and cannabis waste to produce natural insulation materials aimed at improving housing conditions and energy efficiency.

---

## Education & Background

Zaffia studied **Global Studies at Wilfrid Laurier University** and has been passionate about applying indigenous land-based knowledge and biotechnology to address climate change and economic development challenges faced by Indigenous peoples in Canada. 

Her work focuses on creating economic development models that engage Indigenous communities throughout the supply chain to support sovereignty and reconciliation efforts.

## Entrepreneurial Journey

### 🌱 Hempergy (CEO & Founder since 2017)

Founded around 2017, Hempergy addresses the Indigenous housing crisis by using sustainable materials that are:
- Low carbon
- Mold-proof
- Fire-resistant
- Non-toxic

The company uses upcycled hemp and cannabis waste to create natural insulation products aimed at addressing housing and energy-efficiency challenges in Indigenous communities. Hempergy has earned awards like the Youth Innovation Award and has been involved in prototype development and advocacy for Indigenous housing solutions.

**Key Achievement**: Won the **2019 Youth Innovation Award** at the Active Citizens Youth Innovation Summit hosted by the United Nations Association in Canada, which included a **$4,000 funding prize**.

### 🌾 SkyAcres Agrotechnologies (Co-founder and COO)

A startup specializing in modular vertical farming and indoor farming systems that grow organic produce year-round with a focus on sustainability and low water consumption. SkyAcres aims to provide users with underutilized residential and commercial space to grow and sell produce.

**Notable Recognition**:
- Selected for **Google's Women Founders accelerator program** in 2024
- Part of the **RBC Women in Cleantech Accelerator** cohort for 2024–2026
- Raised **$60,000** from the New Ventures BC competition
- Named one of **Canada's Top 30 Under 30 Sustainable Leaders**

### 💼 Laplante Solutions

Zaffia also engages as an independent consultant and founder of Laplante Solutions, working on initiatives around Indigenous entrepreneur support and infrastructure development.
