## 🚀 Quick Start by Role

### For Donors 💝

**First Time?**
1. Visit [SHELTR Platform](https://sheltr-ai.web.app)
2. Click "Donate" or scan QR code
3. Choose donation amount
4. Complete secure payment
5. Receive instant receipt

**Want More?**
- Set up recurring donations
- Track your impact
- Connect with participants
- Join donor community

**See**: [Complete Donor Guide](donor-guide.md)

---

### For Participants 👤

**Getting Started?**
1. Register at a partnered shelter
2. Receive your QR code
3. Access platform features
4. Connect with services
5. Track your progress

**Need Help?**
- Talk to shelter staff
- Use SHELTR chatbot
- Contact support team
- Join participant community

**See**: [Complete Participant Guide](participant-guide.md)

---

### For Shelter Admins 🏢

**Setting Up?**
1. Register your shelter
2. Complete verification
3. Add staff members
4. Import participant data
5. Configure services

**Managing Operations?**
- Track donations
- Manage participants
- Generate reports
- Coordinate with staff
- Monitor metrics

**See**: [Complete Shelter Admin Guide](shelter-admin-guide.md)

## 🎓 Common Tasks

### Making a Donation
1. Choose donation method (QR, online, recurring)
2. Select amount and allocation
3. Complete payment securely
4. Receive instant confirmation
5. Track impact over time

**Time**: 2-3 minutes  
**Guide**: [Donor Guide](donor-guide.md)

---

### Receiving Services
1. Register with partnered shelter
2. Verify identity and eligibility
3. Receive participant QR code
4. Access available services
5. Track your journey

**Time**: 15-30 minutes  
**Guide**: [Participant Guide](participant-guide.md)

---

### Managing a Shelter
1. Complete shelter registration
2. Verify organization details
3. Add staff and participants
4. Configure service offerings
5. Monitor operations

**Time**: 1-2 hours  
**Guide**: [Shelter Admin Guide](shelter-admin-guide.md)

## 🎯 Best Practices

### For Donors 💝

1. **Set up recurring donations** for consistent impact

3. **Share your story** to inspire others
4. **Engage with community** to multiply impact
