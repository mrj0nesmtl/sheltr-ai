### 2.3 Making the First Donation

**Donation Process Flow:**

```
Select Participant → Choose Amount → Add Message (Optional) → 
Select Payment Method → Review Details → Confirm Donation → 
Blockchain Processing → Distribution → Confirmation
```

**Amount Selection:**

**Suggested Amounts:**
- $5: Meal support
- $20: Daily essentials
- $50: Weekly support
- $100: Major milestone support
- Custom: Enter your amount

**Payment Methods:**

✅ **Credit Card** (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Instant processing
- Secure PCI-compliant
- No fees to participant

✅ **Bank Transfer / ACH**
- Lower processing fees
- 2-3 business days
- Ideal for larger amounts

✅ **Apple Pay / Google Pay**
- One-tap convenience
- Mobile optimized
- Instant processing

✅ **Cryptocurrency** (Coming Soon)
- Bitcoin, Ethereum support
- Blockchain-native
- Tax advantages

## 🔧 Common Questions & Support

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is my donation tax-deductible?**  
A: Yes! SHELTR is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. You'll receive instant tax receipts for all donations.

**Q: How much goes directly to participants?**  
A: 80% goes to virtual debit cards, 15% to housing fund with guaranteed returns, 5% to platform operations.

**Q: Can I remain anonymous?**  
A: Yes! You can donate anonymously or use a display name instead of your real name.

**Q: How do I know my donation is making an impact?**  
A: Track real-time impact through your dashboard, blockchain verification, and participant updates.

**Q: Can I donate to multiple participants?**  
A: Absolutely! Support as many participants as you'd like, all tracked in your donor dashboard.

**Q: What if I want to stop recurring donations?**  
A: Cancel anytime with one click from your dashboard. No penalties or fees.

**Q: Can I meet the people I support?**  
A: With participant consent, we organize donor meetups and shelter visits. Safety and privacy are top priorities.

**Q: How secure is my payment information?**  
A: We use bank-level encryption and never store full payment details. All transactions are PCI-DSS compliant.
