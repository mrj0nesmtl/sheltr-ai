## 🔧 Common Questions & Support

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the total cost to government?**  
A: Costs vary by scale. Pilot programs typically range from $100K-$500K. Full implementation depends on population size and service model.

**Q: How does this integrate with existing HMIS systems?**  
A: SHELTR can integrate bidirectionally with most HMIS platforms via API, ensuring data consistency and reducing duplication.

**Q: What data privacy protections are in place?**  
A: Platform is GDPR-compliant, uses encryption, role-based access, and follows all applicable privacy regulations. Government retains data ownership.

**Q: How is success measured?**  
A: Key metrics include housing placements, cost per placement, participant satisfaction, donor engagement, and ROI. Custom metrics available.

**Q: What if the pilot doesn't meet expectations?**  
A: Pilot design includes adjustment protocols, and government can modify, extend, or discontinue based on data and stakeholder input.

**Q: Can this replace existing programs?**  
A: SHELTR complements rather than replaces existing services, enhancing coordination and providing direct support to participants.

**Q: What happens to participant data if partnership ends?**  
A: Government retains all data. Participants can export their information. Transition protocols ensure continuity of care.

**Q: How does this affect our federal/state funding?**  
A: Platform enhances outcomes tracked for federal/state grants and can strengthen applications through improved data and results.
